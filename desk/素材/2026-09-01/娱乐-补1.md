原文：
凭古装剧《琅琊榜》走红的43岁中国女星王鸥，2016年与当时仍是杨幂丈夫的刘恺威合作《莽荒纪》期间，被曝深夜共处一室"对剧本"。事件被网民戏称为"夜光剧本"，而刘恺威的工作人员当时回应，两人只是正常讨论剧本；2021年，王鸥与小四岁的相声演员何九华传出恋情；2023年又被曝怀孕，但她当时未正面回应。

2025年12月，王鸥被拍到带着一名两岁女童逛公园，当时仅称对方是表姐的女儿。近日，她再被拍到与保母一同送女童上课，8月31日松口承认："我目前独自养育一个孩子。她是我生命里很珍贵的礼物，带给我很多温暖与力量。"

三年前，王鸥与相声演员何九华交往期间，男方曾被狗仔拍到在车内向一名圈外女子索吻，随后进入对方住处逗留约一小时，离开后再返回王鸥家，因而被质疑脚踏两条船。当时更有狗仔爆料，王鸥已怀有身孕。

何九华被偷拍后，王鸥始终对感情状况三缄其口，也未曾承认生女。直到三个月前，何九华在节目中自曝育有一名两岁女儿，透露女儿爱看大象、喜欢蓝色，自己也常陪她读唐诗。不过，他全程未提及孩子的母亲。

王鸥如今终于松口承认育有一女，同时希望外界不要过度聚焦她的私人生活，给孩子留下一个安静、自在的成长空间。

原文：
王晶："中国大陆顶流都不会演戏"——他在YouTube频道访谈中指出，中国大陆演员之间出现明显的"两极化"现象：顶流男女主角负责扛起流量，但缺乏演技；反观一众配角，个个演得龙飞凤舞，撑起整部作品的表演质感。

王晶分析问题的症结在于当红艺人忙于应付大量商业活动，经纪公司以他们的时间换取利益，令艺人难以静下心钻研剧本、揣摩角色。他强调这番话并非针对任何特定演员，而是对整个影视产业生态的观察。

## 快讯 · Coyote vs. Acme 北美周末票房 #2 / Spider-Man 逼近 Avatar 2

出处：https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/coyote-vs-acme-box-office-spider-man-titanic-dog-stars-1236685104/ ｜ 日期：2026-08-30
要点：Ketchup Entertainment收购的Looney Tunes真人动画混剪片《Coyote vs. Acme》首周拿下1540万美元、北美3575块银幕，创下公司史上最大首映纪录。Tom Holland主演的《Spider-Man: Brand New Day》第五周蝉联冠军，全球累计23.32亿美元升至影史第4。
原文：
'Coyote vs. Acme' Snags No. 2 With $15.4M as 'Spider-Man' Nears 'Avatar 2'; 'Dog Stars' Put to Sleep — August 30, 2026 9:03am

It might not have been quite the show of emphatic box office support that Looney Tunes fans hoped to see after Coyote vs. Acme avoided becoming a Warner Bros. tax write-off, but the film managed to get rolling over the weekend to score a second-place finish behind the unstoppable Spider-Man: Brand New Day. Meanwhile, Ridley Scott's The Dog Stars was sent to the doghouse.

Ketchup Entertainment released Coyote vs. Acme on 3,575 North American screens, with the film grossing $15.4 million for the weekend after collecting $5.3 million on Friday. Earning an A CinemaScore from audiences, the hybrid of animation and live-action stars Will Forte, Lana Condor and John Cena in the story of Wile E. Coyote suing the Acme Corporation over its faulty products.

This marks the biggest debut ever for the independent distributor, topping their previous best for the Looney Tunes feature The Day the Earth Blew Up, which was originally intended for HBO Max before opening to $3.5 million in March of last year ahead of a $15 million global cume. Coyote vs. Acme had a softer opening than other recent hybrid films — the past 15 of which debuted with an average frame of $23.2 million, according to FranchiseRe — but looks likely to recoup its investment through ancillary markets.

News broke in late 2023 that Warner Bros. had shelved the completed Coyote vs. Acme as a tax write-off. After social media outrage over the fact that director Dave Green's film that carried a roughly $70 million budget would potentially never see the light of day, Ketchup stepped up last year to nab the rights for $50 million. Coyote vs. Acme has impressed critics, earning 95 percent approval from reviewers on Rotten Tomatoes. The Hollywood Reporter critic Frank Scheck deemed the movie "a hilarious delight."

Claiming the top spot for its fifth consecutive weekend was Sony Pictures' Spider-Man: Brand New Day, which swings to $22.2 million to bring its domestic total to $891.5 million. Tom Holland stars in director Destin Daniel Cretton's superhero blockbuster that remains the second-highest-grossing title ever at the domestic box office, behind only 2015's Star Wars: Episode VII — The Force Awakens with $936.6 million.

Brand New Day hit No. 4 at the all-time global box office over the weekend, as its worldwide total now stands at $2.332 billion. This moves 2025's Chinese animated fantasy Ne Zha 2 to No. 5 with $2.27 billion, while James Cameron's 1997 disaster epic Titanic floats to No. 6 with $2.26 billion. Spidey is now just behind 2022's Avatar: The Way of Water, which currently holds third place with $2.334 billion.

As the 2026 summer box office nears its end, this weekend's titles made an estimated total of $97 million for the frame, according to FranchiseRe, which is down from last weekend's $130.5 million. Rentrak expects this summer's titles to have collectively grossed $4.444 billion by Sunday, putting the season 25.5 percent ahead of last year's but in fourth place behind the summers of 2013, 2016 and 2015, in descending order.
